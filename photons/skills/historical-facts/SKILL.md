# Historical Facts

This skill provides historically accurate, engaging facts from different eras and domains whenever requested.

## Fact Categories

- **Ancient History** - Events from ancient civilizations (3000 BCE to 500 CE)
- **Medieval Period** - Facts from medieval times (500 CE to 1500 CE)
- **Renaissance & Early Modern** - 1500-1800
- **Industrial Era** - 1800-1900
- **Modern History** - 1900 onwards
- **Science & Technology** - Scientific discoveries and innovations
- **Culture & Society** - Social movements and cultural events
- **Geography & Exploration** - Discovery and exploration milestones

## Examples

- "Tell me a historical fact"
- "Give me a random historical fact about ancient civilizations"
- "What's an interesting fact from medieval history?"
- "Share a fact about scientific discoveries"
- "Tell me something about the Industrial Revolution"
- "Give me a historical fact about exploration"
- "What's a fascinating historical event from the 20th century?"

## Quality Criteria

Each historical fact should:

- Be concise and impactful (max 512 characters)
- Include: Event/Discovery, Time Period, Location (if applicable), Significance, and Interesting Detail
- Balance comprehensiveness with brevity
- Engage curiosity while respecting character limits

## Usage Pattern

Invoke this skill whenever you want to:

1. Learn something new about history
2. Stimulate creative thinking with historical context
3. Add historical depth to conversations
4. Explore different time periods and cultures
5. Discover connections between historical events

## Example Output Format

**Fact:** [Main historical fact]

**When:** [Time period - specific date if known]

**Where:** [Location/Region]

**Why It Matters:** [Historical significance]

**Did You Know:** [Interesting or surprising detail]

## Instructions for Use

Simply ask for a historical fact in your prompt.  
The skill will select from various historical periods and categories to provide diverse, engaging facts on each invocation.
